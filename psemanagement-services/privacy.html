Privacy

Indiana Fevers Management Services (“IFMS”, psemanagement.services) is a fan-services and operations desk. It is not the official Indiana Fever or WNBA club website.

What we collect

When you submit a form we store the fields you provide (name, email, optional phone, service type, message, requested date) plus a generated request ID and timestamp. Requests are written to Cloudflare KV via /api/inquiry and may also be kept in your browser’s localStorage so you can review them on the Account page.

Payments

Card data is never collected on this site. Checkout uses Stripe Payment Links on the live MANAGEMENT TEAM Stripe account. Stripe’s own privacy terms apply to those pages.

Official team content

News, schedule, roster and ticket links point to public pages on fever.wnba.com and related official Fever/WNBA properties. We do not scrape private accounts or invent box scores.

Cookies

This site uses functional localStorage for request history, cart notes and newsletter flags. See Cookies.
