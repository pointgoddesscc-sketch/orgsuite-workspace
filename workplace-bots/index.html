Architecture rule: these bots use workplace OAuth connectors (GitHub, Linear, Vercel, Gmail, Outlook, Calendar, Calendly, Teams, Notion, Figma, Canva, Stripe, Automations). They do not store website passwords and they do not run a cloud computer that signs into vendor portals. Irreversible actions stay in an approval queue.
